                        Choose your DNS resolver
                        
      
                        
        DNS reveals site names to the resolver you pick. This does NOT hide
        browsing from your internet provider. Use a VPN or Tor for that.
      
                        
      
      
                        
        
                        
          
          
        
                        
        
      
                        
      
                        
        
                        
        
                        
        
        
                        Compare
                        
        
                        
          
                        System
                        
          
                        
            Default. Nothing changes.
            Uses an active VPN's resolver; otherwise your internet provider's. This browser leaves lookups unencrypted, letting a provider log what you look up and, in some places, sell it. Blocks nothing, and supports public WiFi sign-in.
          
                        
          
                        Quad9
                        
          
                        
            Encrypted. Blocks malware.
            Swiss nonprofit. Encrypted. Blocks only malware and phishing, so it rarely breaks a page. States it validates signed answers (DNSSEC) and sends none of your IP address to queried servers. Sees every domain you look up, and never falls back to network DNS, so public WiFi sign-in needs System.
          
                        
        
                        
        
                        
          Encrypted does not mean unseen. Quad9 sees every site you look up.
        
                        
        
                        
          The site name is encrypted when the site supports it, but the IP
          address of the server you connect to remains visible. If the lookup
          itself is unencrypted, your network can interfere with it and strip
          away the information needed to enable that protection.
        
                        
        
                        
          Quad9 never falls back to network DNS, because a fallback would hand
          your lookups to the network in the open. That breaks public WiFi
          sign-in. On hotel or airport WiFi, switch to System, restart, and
          sign in.

                            Encrypted export
                            
          
                            
            Move or back up the vault in one encrypted file protected by a new passphrase. This covers a lost machine, not a forgotten passphrase. Only the recovery key does.
          
                            
          
                            
            
                            
              
            
                            
            
            
            
            
                            
              
            
                            
            
                            
            
                            
          
                            

          
                            Import a backup
                            
          
          
                            
            Importing
            replaces the vault on this machine. Every password, note, and bookmark here is replaced by the backup. There is no undo. Export anything the backup does not contain first.
          
                            
          
                            
            Choose a new vault passphrase and recovery key. The export
            passphrase will open only the backup file.
          
                            
          
                            
            
                            
              
            
                            
            
                            
            
            
            
            
            
                            
              
            
                            
            
                            
          
                            

          
                            Change passphrase
                            
          
          
                            Changing the passphrase is not available in this version. Your current passphrase and your recovery key keep working. It returns in the next update.
                            
          
          
                            
            Your contents and recovery key do not change. If this fails, your old passphrase still works.
          
                            
          
                            
            
            
            
            
                            
              
            
                            
            
                            
            
                            
          
                            

          
                            Plaintext export: the way out
                            
          
                            
            This writes every password and note into an unencrypted file. Backups, search tools, or the disk may keep readable copies after you delete it.
          
                            
          
                            
            Use this to leave PATANYX.
